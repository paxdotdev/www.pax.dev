Ship to any device²

Pax is written in Rust and runs on metal, interfacing as directly as practical with the target's CPU, GPU, and memory.

This enables Pax to be highly portable and performant, achieving up to 240FPS rendering, runnable natively on practically any computer or mobile device.

Pax's primary design challenge is to offer high-level ergonomics, inspired by HTML & CSS among others, while prioritizing portability, performance, and resource efficiency.

Free & open source

Pax started as a sabbatical project by Zack Brown, then became incubated through a company along with Warfa Jibril and Samuel Selleck, backed by South Park Commons and some other cool people.

Pax's long-term mission is two-fold: (1) to empower art on screens and (2) to enable more of humanity to create software.

Pax is licensed MIT or Apache 2.0, at your choice.
