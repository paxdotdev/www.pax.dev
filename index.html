Design-native

Pax enables AI collaboration on a visual canvas, alongside a codebase copilot.

Native-native

Pax projects build into pure native apps or lightning fast web apps, thanks to modern technologies: WASM, WebGPU / WebGL, and Rust. Pax’s example harnesses render beyond 240 frames per second, and we have room to grow.
